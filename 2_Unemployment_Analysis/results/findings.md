# Key findings

- Cleaned **740** state-area observations from **2019-05-31** to **2020-06-30**.
- The unweighted mean across observed state-area rates was **9.51%** before March 2020 and **17.78%** from March through June 2020.
- The observed April and May 2020 means were **23.64%** and **24.88%**.

These are unweighted averages of state-area rows, not official national estimates. The timing is descriptive and does not establish causation. The 14-month window is too short to determine recurring annual seasonality.
